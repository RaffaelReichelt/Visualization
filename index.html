🎯 Gamma-Ray Burst Search (FGRP5)

Your computers are searching for continuous gravitational waves from gamma-ray burst sources. These signals would be emitted by rapidly spinning neutron stars that were formed during a supernova explosion associated with a gamma-ray burst.

📊 Data Analysis

The search analyzes LIGO detector data, looking for nearly-periodic signals that slowly change frequency over time. The search covers a frequency range around 56 Hz - within LIGO's most sensitive band.

🔬 Computational Method

The FGRP5 pipeline uses a stack-slide method to search for signals with unknown frequency evolution. Each workunit tests millions of potential signal templates across the parameter space.
